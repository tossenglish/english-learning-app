---
name: Reading comprehension AI boundaries
description: Teacher-authored reading questions and privacy-safe Claude feedback rules.
---

Teachers author and save all reading questions. Claude is limited to explaining objective answers and grading short answers against the teacher's answer and explanation note; do not generate student questions with AI or send member identity.

**Why:** The teacher must control the correct answer, and student-facing feedback must remain grounded in the passage without sharing personal information.

**How to apply:** Keep objective grading deterministic, keep Claude inputs limited to passage/question/answer context, and preserve the per-student daily AI limit and cached explanations.
