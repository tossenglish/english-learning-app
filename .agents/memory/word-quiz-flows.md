---
name: Separate word quiz flows
description: Debugging guidance for the home quick check and course assignment word practice.
---

The home quick check and course assignment practice are separate flows with different content sources and progression state; fixing one does not fix the other. In assignments, word practice should use word-specific modes (word writing and an English meaning quiz), hide sentence-only passage panels, avoid duplicate instructions, play the word pronunciation after a correct answer in either mode before advancing, and retain typing focus in writing mode. Both word and sentence practice give distinct sound feedback for correct and incorrect answers.

**Why:** A prior fix changed only course assignment progression while the learner also experienced the same issue in the home quick check; the user also asked for a more efficient word-specific assignment flow.

**How to apply:** Verify the active route, word source, correct/incorrect sounds in both practice types, audio and automatic next-word behavior in both word modes, and focus on the next writing input before calling a word-progression issue resolved. Keep word-writing/meaning-quiz behavior separate from sentence practice modes.
