---
name: Separate word quiz flows
description: Debugging guidance for the home quick check and course assignment word practice.
---

When a learner reports that a word quiz will not advance, check both the home quick check and course assignment practice. They are separate flows with different content sources and progression state; fixing one does not fix the other.

**Why:** A prior fix changed only course assignment progression while the learner also experienced the same issue in the home quick check.

**How to apply:** Verify the active route, word source, answer feedback, and next-word control on both screens before calling a word-progression issue resolved.
