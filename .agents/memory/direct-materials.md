name: Direct learning materials
description: Directly entered study content should remain deterministic and not depend on AI translation.
---

Directly entered material uses an explicit type and optional English-to-Korean pairs so admins can control exactly what members study without an AI request.

**Why:** The user needs predictable, user-authored content and should not be charged for translation calls when entering material manually.

**How to apply:** Keep direct sentence/word entry separate from uploaded-file translation; preserve line order and support `English||Korean` entries.
